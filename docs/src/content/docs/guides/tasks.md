---
title: Tasks
description: How the daily task list works, from quick capture to rescheduling what did not get done.
---

Tasks are the work you owe someone. AlphOne opens on the day's list, and
that list is the main screen of the product: work it top to bottom and
postpone whatever did not happen.

## The day

The **Tasks** screen shows one day at a time. The chevrons either side of
the heading move to the previous and next day, and a **Today** link
appears whenever you are looking at another day.

Everything on the screen belongs to you. Tasks are owned by the person
they are assigned to, and the day list only shows your own.

Higher priority tasks come first. Within one priority, tasks with a time of
day come before those without, the earliest time first. Tasks with no time
keep the order they were added.

A task with a time of day shows that time before its title, in a column of
its own, so the hours line up down the list like a calendar agenda. A task
with no time leaves the column blank. Every task list works this way: the
day, the **Done** group, **Overdue** and a contact's tasks. Times are
written the way your workspace writes them, such as 09:30 or 09:30 AM.

## Adding work

The **Task title** field above the list adds a task to the day on screen.
Type a title and press Enter, or pick a due date, a time and a priority
first. That is the fast path, meant for capturing work while you are on the
phone.

The **Time** field is optional. Leave it empty for work that only has to
happen some time that day. After each task it goes back to empty, like the
priority goes back to normal.

The **New task** button at the top of the screen opens the full form,
with the same **Time** field next to **Due date**, where a task can also
get a contact.
Search for the person by name and pick them, and the task is linked to
them from both sides. **Add task** in a contact's row of the contact list
opens the same form with that contact already picked.

## Completing

**Complete** moves a task into the **Done** group at the bottom of the
day, which stays collapsed until you open it. Completed work is kept in
view rather than disappearing, so a misclick is one **Reopen** away and
the finished pile is visible at the end of the day.

## Overdue work

Open tasks from earlier days appear in an **Overdue** section above
today's list, each showing the date it was originally due. Higher priority
work from every earlier day comes first, and work of the same priority lists
oldest first, each day in the same time order as today's list. They are never
folded silently into today. Moving them forward is something you do, not
something the app does behind your back.

## Rescheduling

**Postpone** moves an open task one day later. A task due today, or overdue
for a week, lands on tomorrow, never on a date that is still in the past. A
task already due on a later day moves to the day after it. This is the
no-answer pattern: call, no reply, postpone, and it comes back tomorrow.
Postpone changes the day only, so a task due at 09:30 is still due at 09:30
on its new day.

For any other date or time, open the task and edit its **Due date** or
**Time** field directly. To take the time off a task, empty the **Time**
field and press **Save**.

## Tasks on a contact

A contact's page lists the open tasks linked to that person, across
everyone on the team, higher priority first, and has its own form for adding
one. It answers the question you have while looking at somebody: what do we
owe them.

Type a title, pick the day it is due, a time if it has one and its
priority, and press **Add task**. The date starts on today and goes back to
today after each task, so a follow-up for Friday takes one extra click, not
a trip to the task's own page. The time goes back to empty after each task.

Each task on the page shows its time before the title and the date it is
due after it.
