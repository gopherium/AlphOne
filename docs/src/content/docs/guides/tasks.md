---
title: Tasks
description: How the daily task list works, from quick capture to rescheduling what did not get done.
---

Tasks are the work you owe someone. AlphOne opens on the day's list, and
that list is the main screen of the product: work it top to bottom and
push forward whatever did not happen.

## The day

The **Tasks** screen shows one day at a time. The chevrons either side of
the heading move to the previous and next day, and a **Today** link
appears whenever you are looking at another day.

Everything on the screen belongs to you. Tasks are owned by the person
they are assigned to, and the day list only shows your own.

## Adding work

The field above the list adds a task to the day on screen. Type a title
and press Enter. That is the fast path, meant for capturing work while
you are on the phone.

**New task** opens the full form, where a task also gets a priority and
an optional contact. Search for the person by name and pick them, and the
task is linked to them from both sides.

## Completing

**Complete** moves a task into the **Done** group at the bottom of the
day, which stays collapsed until you open it. Completed work is kept in
view rather than disappearing, so a misclick is one **Reopen** away and
the finished pile is visible at the end of the day.

## Overdue work

Open tasks from earlier days appear in an **Overdue** section above
today's list, each showing the date it was originally due. They are never
folded silently into today. Moving them forward is something you do, not
something the app does behind your back.

## Rescheduling

**Push to tomorrow** moves a task to the day after today, whatever it was
due before. A task that has been overdue for a week lands on tomorrow,
not on a date that is still in the past. This is the no-answer pattern:
call, no reply, push, and it comes back tomorrow.

For any other date, open the task and edit its due date directly.

## Tasks on a contact

A contact's page lists the open tasks linked to that person, across
everyone on the team, and has its own field for adding one. It answers
the question you have while looking at somebody: what do we owe them.
